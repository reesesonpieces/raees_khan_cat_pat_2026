Focus Question

What are the benefits and ethical issues of using Artificial Intelligence (AI) in healthcare?

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is technology that allows computers to perform tasks that normally need human intelligence.

Uses of AI in Healthcare

  • Finding diseases
  • Checking medical scans
  • Helping doctors with treatment
  • Managing patient information

Benefits of AI

  • Faster diagnosis
  • Better patient care
  • Saves time
  • Can reduce human errors

Ethical Issues

  • Patient privacy
  • Data security
  • AI bias
  • AI mistakes
